Thread

A Quiet Puzzle · iOS

Sort colored dots into matching rows. Rotate and connect pipe segments to form a path through the board. When everything lines up, trace the thread — and feel it click.

  • Two puzzles in one: sort the colors and connect the thread
  • Infinite procedurally generated puzzles, always solvable
  • Seven color palettes inspired by the Pacific Northwest
  • Original ambient soundtrack composed for the game
  • No ads. No timers. No pressure.

About

Petrichor Games is a one-person independent studio making small, considered games for people who need a moment.

Based in the Pacific Northwest. Named for the smell of rain on dry earth. Making games that feel like that.
