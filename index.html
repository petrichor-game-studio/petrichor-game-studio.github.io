Thread

A Quiet Puzzle

Two puzzles stacked on top of each other. Sort the colored dots into matching rows, then rotate the pipe pieces until they line up into one unbroken path. Get both right and the thread runs clean from the top of the board to the bottom.

  • Endless procedurally generated boards
  • Five difficulty tiers and an endless mode, with the swap count tightening as you go
  • Seven color palettes drawn from the Pacific Northwest
  • An original ambient soundtrack, written for the game
  • No ads, no timers, no accounts, no notifications
